CarrotBox

CarrotBox is BeepBox (by John Nesky, MIT license) with a playlist view, samples, sound kits, FL Studio style effects and a set of plugins, while keeping BeepBox's simple layout. Everything you knew from BeepBox still works and old song links still open.

What's new

  • Two views. The View button (or F5) switches between BeepBox's pattern grid and a Playlist: one continuous timeline whose clips show their notes. Ctrl+scroll zooms, Alt+scroll changes track height, drag clips to move them (Ctrl/⌘-drag copies), drag a clip's right edge to repeat it, right-click deletes, double-click makes a pattern and dragging in the ruler sets the loop.
  • Single notes in chords. Drag the end of one note inside a chord to change only that note. Notes may overlap like in a piano roll. Hold Alt while dragging to ignore snapping.
  • Ghost notes. The ghost button above the piano roll shows the other channels' notes behind the current one. Alt+click a ghost note to jump to its channel.
  • Samples. Sampler, Slicex (chop a loop across the keyboard), FPC (12 drum pads) and 3x Osc, plus a Sound Browser (F8) with hundreds of built-in sounds and your own kits (folders, files or .zip).
  • Plugins (Tab): Swarm (hybrid synth), Prism (wavetable synth), Seedling (grow sounds from DNA), Chop Shop (sample chopper with key and tempo matching), Sketchpad (draw melodies, build chords, bass and arps) and Mangler FX (macro effect rack). Install the ones you want in the Plugin Manager.
  • Effects. Parametric EQ 2, Gross Beat and Soundgoodizer on any instrument or on the master (F9).
  • Tools. Lead / melody generator (G), audio recorder, metronome, color themes and an optional modern skin. Press ? for every shortcut.
  • Saving. Song URLs remember everything except audio files. Use File > Save Project + Samples to make a .json that includes your samples, and drop that file on the page to open it.

BeepBox basics

Click the gray rows to add notes, and click above or below a note to make a chord. Drag horizontally on a note to change its length, vertically to bend its pitch. The numbered boxes (or playlist clips) choose which pattern plays in each bar.

  • Space: play/pause, Shift+Space: play from the mouse
  • Z/Y: undo/redo, C/V: copy/paste
  • 0-9: set pattern number, Enter: insert bar, Backspace: delete bars
  • [/]: move playhead, F5: switch view, F8: sound browser, F9: master effects, Tab: plugins, Esc: close the top window

Built-in sounds are generated by this page; no FL Studio content is included. The plugins are original CarrotBox plugins inspired by the workflows of popular commercial ones. BeepBox and CarrotBox are MIT licensed.
